Frequently Asked Questions

Why is UEFI Global free compared to traditional institutions?
Our model is supported by global partnerships, EdTech coalition funding, and a commitment to democratize education without the heavy institutional overhead.
What courses are available in the CBE Tracks?
We offer comprehensively aligned curriculums for Grade 9 through 12, focusing on STEM, Arts, Sports Science, and Advanced Tech Bootcamps.
Are these courses accredited?
Yes, our completion certificates are mapped to competency-based structural requirements and are recognized by our partner university network.
What devices can I watch on?
UEFI Global is fully responsive. You can learn on desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones seamlessly.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes, our Progressive Web App (PWA) allows you to install UEFI Global directly to your phone's home screen for offline access.
How do I track my progress throughout a course?
Your learner dashboard provides real-time analytics, module completion checkmarks, and automated competency evaluations.
Are there live masterclass sessions?
Absolutely. While core modules are pre-recorded for flexibility, we host monthly live webinars and Q&A sessions with our faculty.
How does the mentorship program work?
Enrolled students can apply for 1-on-1 mentorship pairings based on their career goals, receiving tailored guidance from industry professionals.
Can I switch my CBE track halfway through?
Yes, our platform allows modular flexibility. You can pivot tracks and retain credits for overlapping fundamental courses.
What is included in the Tech Hub?
The Tech Hub grants access to coding environments, cloud hosting credits, software licenses, and advanced bootcamps for web/app development.
Do you provide university placement assistance?
Our University Prep track includes dedicated advisors who assist with essay writing, portfolio building, and scholarship applications globally.
Are there any hidden fees for certificates?
No. Generating digital, verifiable certificates upon successful course completion is entirely covered by our partner funding model.
How can organizations partner with UEFI Global?
Organizations can use the 'Partner with Us' dropdown in the navigation menu to explore corporate sponsorship and tech-provision pipelines.
How does the 24/7 AI Tutoring work?
Our proprietary AI model is trained on our curriculum context and can instantly clarify concepts, provide practice quizzes, and explain equations anytime.
Is there a community forum for students?
Yes, the curated social space allows learners to form study groups, discuss complex topics, and collaborate on open-source projects safely.
How are exams and assessments conducted?
Assessments utilize a mix of automated randomized quizzes, peer-reviewed essays, and practical project submissions graded by our educators.
Are certificates physical or digital?
Certificates are primarily issued as secure, blockchain-verified digital credentials, though printable PDFs are provided.
What happens if I forget my account password?
You can easily reset your password using the 'Forgot Password' link on the sign-in modal, or log in securely using your Google account.
Do you support accessibility for students with disabilities?
Yes, our platform includes closed captioning, screen-reader compatibility, high-contrast modes, and variable playback speeds.
Can parents track their student's performance?
Parents or guardians can link their accounts to a student profile to receive weekly automated reports on module completion and assessment scores.
