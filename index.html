Frequently Asked Questions

Why is UEFI Global free compared to traditional institutions?
Our model is supported by global partnerships, EdTech coalition funding, and a commitment to democratize education without the heavy institutional overhead.
What courses are available in the CBE Tracks?
We offer comprehensively aligned curriculums for Grade 9 through 12, focusing on STEM, Arts, Sports Science, and Advanced Tech Bootcamps.
Are these courses accredited?
Yes, our completion certificates are mapped to competency-based structural requirements and are recognized by our partner university network.
What devices can I watch on?
UEFI Global is fully responsive. You can learn on desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones seamlessly.
Is there a mobile app?
Yes, our Progressive Web App (PWA) allows you to install UEFI Global directly to your phone's home screen for offline access.
