Grounded in Reality: The Data

We partner with the ideals of the United Nations to bring you accurate data. Knowing where we stand is the first step to changing the trajectory.

According to UN Women and the Kenya Women's Empowerment Index (WEI), only 29% to 40.6% of women are currently considered fully empowered to participate equally in political and economic life. Furthermore, as of 2024, only 23.3% of seats in parliament are held by women. Employment rates show a stark contrast: 34% for women compared to 47% for men.

This platform exists to shatter those statistics permanently.

My Muse

History is written by those who refuse to be silent. Here we profile the giants whose shoulders we stand upon, and the modern icons currently reshaping the globe.

Tech Innovator & CEO

Dr. Amina Kenyatta

Starting with a single laptop in Thika, Dr. Kenyatta built one of East Africa's largest Agritech platforms, connecting over 200,000 female farmers directly to global markets. She completely bypassed predatory middlemen, increasing rural women's income by an average of 340%.

Her Philosophy: "Code is just a tool. The real innovation is empathy. When you understand the pain points of the most marginalized, your software becomes revolutionary."

Global Policy Advocate

Sarah Ndlovu

Drafting key amendments for the UN Sustainable Development Goal 5, Sarah's legal frameworks have been adopted by 14 nations. She focuses specifically on the intersection of climate change and female economic vulnerability.

Her Philosophy: "Do not wait for a seat at the table. Build your own table, bring your own chairs, and draft your own legislation."
