The Reality & The Mission

We do not operate on platitudes; we operate on data. The UEFI GO GIRL platform is built in alignment with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 5 (SDG5) to achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls.

The numbers dictate our urgency. According to the UN Women and the Kenya Women's Empowerment Index (WEI), 40.6 per cent of women in Kenya are considered empowered in 2025, an improvement from 29.3 per cent in 2020. Yet, this means nearly 60% of women still face severe barriers to economic, political, and cultural participation. Furthermore, as of February 2024, only 23.3% of seats in the Kenyan parliament are held by women.

This platform exists to shatter those barriers. Through relentless education in technology, financial literacy, and mental fortitude, we are building the next generation of female leaders, coders, and wealth creators.

The Blueprint of Resilience

Consider the journey from a rural setup to parliament. The data shows empowerment rates are drastically lower in rural areas. Overcoming this requires immense strategy.

  • 1. Education as Leverage As the WEI reports, women in households with post-secondary education are significantly more empowered. "How She Did It" often starts with aggressive pursuit of knowledge.
  • 2. Strategic Networking Building alliances across industries. Mentorship isn't just about advice; it's about sponsorship in rooms you haven't entered yet.
  • 3. Navigating Policy Understanding legal frameworks to protect assets and rights, utilizing available legislation to secure business interests and personal safety.
