Grounded in Reality: The Gender Data

We partner with the ideals of the United Nations to bring you accurate data regarding women's empowerment. Knowing where we stand is the first step to changing the trajectory.

According to UN Women and the Kenya Women's Empowerment Index (WEI), only about 29% to 40.6% of women in Kenya are currently considered fully empowered to participate equally in political, economic, and cultural life. Furthermore, as of February 2024, only 23.3% of seats in the Kenyan parliament are held by women. Among working-age adults, employment rates show a stark contrast: 34% for women compared to 47% for men.

This platform exists to shatter those statistics. Through tech education, financial mastery, and powerful mentorship, we are increasing that percentage, one girl at a time.

The Universal 3-Phase Process

01. Audit

Every success story began with a ruthless audit of current circumstances. Assessing available capital, identifying critical knowledge gaps, and unapologetically cutting toxic ties that drained mental bandwidth.

02. Acquisition

The aggressive pursuit of leverage. Whether it was returning to university, finding high-level mentors, or self-teaching Python and financial literacy at 2 AM. You must acquire the tools before you build.

03. Deployment

Execution. Launching the campaign, deploying the code, starting the business, or negotiating the executive position. Moving permanently from theoretical knowledge to applied, undeniable power.

Why Tech Needs You

The tech industry fundamentally shapes how humanity operates, yet women remain severely underrepresented. In Kenya, while we see strides in general literacy (91% for women), post-secondary education disparities still limit women's entry into high-tech engineering and software development roles. We are here to change that.

Learning to code isn't just about computers; it's about problem-solving, logic, and building scalable solutions. From Python to JavaScript, AI to Blockchain, the tools of tomorrow must be wielded by diverse minds to prevent algorithmic bias and create inclusive products.
