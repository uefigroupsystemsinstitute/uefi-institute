Influential Women Magazine

The official magazine of Influential Women — leadership, inspiration, and authentic stories written by women leaders.

Influential Women Magazine is the publication dedicated to amplifying women's voices and showcasing the impact of women leaders across industries. Launching this fall, the magazine is written entirely by professional women who share their knowledge, experiences, and journeys of triumph. Every article in Influential Women Magazine is authored by women in business, women in technology, women in healthcare, women in law, and women in creative fields.

A magazine written by women leaders for women leaders

Influential Women Magazine is unique because it is not written by outsiders. Every feature is created by members of the Influential Women community. These contributors include executives, entrepreneurs, innovators, authors, and professionals from diverse sectors. The magazine covers leadership, executive presence, business growth, personal branding, and the authentic voices of women who are creating change.

One of the most popular features of Influential Women is the How She Did It series. In these articles, women leaders share powerful quotes and insights about success, authenticity, resilience, and leadership. This same spirit of inspiration will carry into Influential Women Magazine.

What readers will discover in the first issue

  • Executive presence and leadership communication: Learn proven strategies for building influence and authority. A preview can be found in our executive presence article.
  • Personal journeys of triumph: Real stories from women who have turned challenges into opportunities and setbacks into success.
  • Authentic leadership and thought leadership: Articles that highlight the unique ways women in leadership are transforming industries.
  • Exclusive cover feature: A world renowned leader in finance and empowerment will be revealed on the debut cover of Influential Women Magazine.
